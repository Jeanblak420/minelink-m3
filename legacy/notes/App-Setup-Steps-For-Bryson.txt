

Employee Joining Workflow

Here is the breakdown of the employee joining process:

Step 1 - Employee Action: Navigate Post-Signup

-The employee is automatically navigated to the company selection screen after signing up.

Step 2 - Employee Action: Select Company

-The employee selects their intended company, [Company Name].

Step 3 - System Action: Send Request to Admin, Management, or Company users

-The system automatically sends an approval request notification to the designated users for [Company Name].

Step 4 - System Action: Notify Employee of Pending Status

-Simultaneously, the system displays a message to the employee: "Welcome! Thank you for registering with us. Your request to join [Company Name] as an employee has been sent to Management for approval and role assignment. Once management has approved your request and assigned you a role, you'll receive a notification message here. Please check back periodically."

-Employee access is limited to the profile feature or Logout option.

-This pending status message and limited access persist if the employee logs out and logs back in before the designated users for [Company Name] action is completed.

Step 5 - Admin, Management, or Company users Action: Review Request

-Admin, Management, or Company users for [Company Name] receive and review the employee's join request.

Step 6 - Admin, Management, or Company users Action: Make Decision

-Admin, Management, or Company users must choose one of two actions: Approve or Decline.

Step 7 - System Action (Handling Decline): Notify Employee

If Admin, Management, or Company users selects Decline:

-The system sends a notification message to the employee: "Oops! [Company Name] has declined your request to join their company. If this was an error, please contact management or re-select the company you are employed with."

Step 8 - System Action (Handling Decline): Await Employee Acknowledgement

-The system waits for the employee to acknowledge the decline message (e.g., by clicking a "Got It" button).

Step 9 - System Action (Handling Decline): Redirect Employee

After the employee acknowledges the decline:

-The system automatically redirects the employee back to the company selection screen (Return to Step 2).

Step 10 - System Action (Handling Approval): Prompt for Role

If Admin, Management, or Company users selects Approve:

-The system prompts the Admin, Management, or Company users to assign a specific role to the employee within [Company Name].

Step 11 - Admin, Management, or Company users Action (Handling Approval): Assign Role

Following the prompt (if approved):

-Admin, Management, or Company users selects and confirms the role for the employee.-Admin, Management, or Company users receives a notification which confirms the role assigned to employee.

Step 12 - System Action (Handling Approval): Notify Employee of Success

Once Admin, Management, or Company users has successfully approved and assigned a role:

-The system sends a final notification message to the employee confirming successful joining and their assigned role (Note: The current message we have in place which starts with ‘Congratulations!‘).

Step 13 - System Outcome: Activate Employee

-The employee is now considered an active member of [Company Name] within the system.

-The employee gains the permissions associated with their assigned role.

-The employee can now proceed to use the application's features relevant to their role.
