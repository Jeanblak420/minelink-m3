## WORKFLOW PROCESSES

### Company Registration Flow
1. User selects "Sign up as Company"
2. Provides company details (name, contact info, organization type)
3. Creates account with email and password
4. Uploads company logo (optional)
5. Account is created and user is logged in
6. Company dashboard is displayed with appropriate tabs based on organization type

### Employee Registration Flow
1. User selects "Sign up as Employee"
2. Provides personal details (name, contact info)
3. Creates account with email and password
4. Uploads profile photo and required documents
5. Account is created and user is logged in
6. Employee is prompted to select a company to join
7. Request is sent to the selected company
8. Employee remains in "pending" status until approved

### Company-Employee Linking Process
1. Employee selects a company to join
2. Company receives a notification of the join request
3. Company admin can approve or decline the request
4. If approved, employee is linked to the company
5. Company can assign a role to the employee
6. Employee receives a notification of role assignment
7. Employee gains access to role-specific features

### Employee Dismissal Process
1. Company initiates dismissal with reason
2. Employee receives notification and can submit appeal
3. Company reviews appeal and makes final decision
4. If dismissal is upheld, account is scheduled for deletion
5. If dismissal is overturned, employee status is restored

### Fleet Vehicle Management Process
1. Admin adds new vehicle with registration details
2. Vehicle appears in active fleet list
3. Admin can assign a driver to the vehicle
4. Driver documents are linked to the vehicle
5. Admin can mark vehicle as inactive when needed
6. Inactive vehicles can be reactivated

### Weighbridge Operation Process
1. Controller/WB user logs into the system
2. Views weighbridge statistics dashboard
3. Initiates new weighing with truck and driver details
4. Records weight in tons
5. System automatically calculates revenue based on configured rates
6. Generates weighing receipt
7. Updates statistics and history
8. Data is available for reporting and analytics

### Security Check Process
1. Security personnel logs into the system
2. Views security dashboard with active personnel and incidents
3. Records gate checks and security incidents
4. Verifies driver and vehicle documentation
5. Approves or denies entry based on verification
6. Logs all security activities
7. Generates security reports

### Mineral Tracking Process
1. Mine/Supplier records mineral extraction details
2. Weighbridge confirms mineral weight
3. System tracks mineral type, quality, and quantity
4. Buyer receives notification of available minerals
5. Transaction is recorded when minerals are purchased
6. Revenue is calculated based on configured rates
7. Reports are generated for all parties

### Mineral Purchase Process
1. Buyer browses available minerals on map
2. Selects mineral and quantity to purchase
3. System generates proforma invoice
4. Buyer submits payment or proof of payment
5. Mine/Supplier approves payment
6. System generates final invoice
7. Mineral quantity is updated in inventory
8. Transaction is recorded in purchase history
