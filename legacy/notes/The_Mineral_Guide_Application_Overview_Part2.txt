### Data Models
- AppUser: Extended user model with role and type information
- Company: Organization data model with organization type and contact details
- Employee: User profile with company association, role, and status
- Notification: System and user notifications with metadata and importance levels
- FleetVehicle: Vehicle management data including registration and driver assignment
- EmployeeStatusHistory: Historical employee status tracking for audit purposes
- WeighbridgeRecord: Weighing data including truck, driver, and tonnage information
- MineralLoad: Tracking of mineral loads with type, weight, and quality metrics
- DocumentReference: Storage of document URLs with verification status
- ActionRecord: Detailed tracking of user actions for audit purposes
- BroadcastMessage: System for cross-client communication
- RevenueRecord: Immutable financial records for revenue tracking
- SecurityIncident: Tracking of security incidents and resolutions
- GateCheck: Recording vehicle entry/exit and document verification
- UserRole: Enumeration of available user roles with permissions
- OrgType: Enumeration of organization types with specific capabilities
- NotificationType: Classification of different notification categories
- EmployeeStatus: Tracking of employee status transitions
- DocumentType: Classification of document types for storage and verification
